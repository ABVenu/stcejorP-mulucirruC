Typical Foundation Plan

Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday are example days only. They can be changed. Before the course starts, two videos land on two different days. After Virtual Inaugural, two videos release each week until all Foundation videos are open.

  1. Beginner library — 30 sessions, never skipped. Expert Path uses the same cadence with Expert-level sessions.
  2. Before the course starts — 2 sessions per week on separate days.
  3. Virtual Inaugural — the live course opens.
  4. Same week repeats through the 6-month course, then End of Course. Leftover Foundation videos always finish inside those 6 months — they do not extend the course.
