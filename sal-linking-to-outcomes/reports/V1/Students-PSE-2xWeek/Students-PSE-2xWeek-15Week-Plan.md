# Students · PSE Focus · 2 sessions/week · 15 weeks

**Persona:** Students · **Cadence:** 2 sessions/week · **Duration:** 15 weeks · **Sessions:** 30  
**Focus:** Placement bar — **Python + SQL + Excel**. **No ML** (not enough time after PSE fluency).  
**Not a cut of the 3x PSE plan** — slower weekly load with a complete pillar sequence.

### Pedagogy
| Weeks | Pillar | Sessions | Intent |
|------:|--------|---------:|--------|
| 1–7 | Python (+ light numbers) | 14 | Solid coding fluency before databases |
| 8–11 | SQL | 8 | Core analyst SQL path (filter→agg→joins→subquery/CTE→window) |
| 12–15 | Excel | 8 | Interface→functions→lookup→pivot→dashboard→prep/revision |

Compared to 3x PSE: fewer advanced SQL topics (no LAG/NTILE/normalization/ER deep-dive) and less Excel repetition — but every pillar still reaches a usable placement bar.
