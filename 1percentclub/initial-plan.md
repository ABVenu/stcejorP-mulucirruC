# Initial plan

Handpick a high bar, then put those students on real problems from hiring partners. The whole exercise is 2 months. The first 1.5 months are the work. The last half month is the perk.

## 1. Screening

Target 100 students. All three bars must be cleared.

| Bar | Cut |
| --- | --- |
| Assignment | More than 90% |
| Attendance | More than 90% |
| Eval score | More than 90% |

## 2. Invite and hackathon

Send the 100 an invite to a hackathon.

- They work on a problem statement.
- Mentors assess the work.
- Students who clear that assessment are selected.
- Selection is the start of the real-life problem statements. The hackathon is the gate, not the program.

## 3. The 1.5 months of work

Hiring partners bring their own problem statements. Selected students work on those.

| Item | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 1.5 months |
| Problem statements | 3 to 4 |
| Work | Once a week |
| Assessment | Once every 2 weeks, by mentors and by the companies |

Each assessment is a push to the next problem. Students who hold the bar move forward. The point of the work is to impress the companies.

## 4. The last half month: perks

The final two weeks are for the outcome, not another problem statement.

| Who | Perk |
| --- | --- |
| Students | Job. Impress the company on its own problem and get hired. |
| Senior folks | A job may not be the right outcome. Growth and networking. |
| Anyone who wants to build | Startup ideas and funding. |

## 5. Calendar

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Before month 1 | Screen to 100. Assignment, attendance, and eval score, each above 90%. |
| Hackathon | Invite the 100. One problem statement. Mentors assess. Select who continues. |
| Month 1 to middle of month 2 | 3 to 4 real problem statements from hiring partners. Work once a week. Assessment every 2 weeks by mentors and companies. |
| Last half month | Jobs, networking, and, for those who want it, a startup pitch and funding. |
| Total | 2 months |
