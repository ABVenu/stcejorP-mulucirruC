title: Python — Sets, Tuples & Dictionaries

objective: Use tuples as fixed packs, sets for unique values, and dictionaries for labelled lookup, and choose among them and a list.

topics be covered:
tuples ordered and immutable; packing and unpacking; sets unique and unordered; add; remove; intersection; union; difference; dictionaries key-value; get; keys; values; items; update; loop; choosing list vs tuple vs set vs dict

detailed subtopics to be covered:
Create a tuple, read it by index, and write a one-item tuple with a comma
Pack values into a tuple and unpack them in order
Build a set, add and remove values, and start from set()
Combine sets with & | and -
Store key-value pairs and read them with square brackets and get
Walk keys, values, and items, and merge with update
Choose list, tuple, set, or dictionary with a short comparison
Do not re-teach list methods
