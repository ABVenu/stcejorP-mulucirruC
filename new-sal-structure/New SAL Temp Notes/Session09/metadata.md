title: AI — Introduction to GenAI & LLMs

objective: Explain AI, generative AI, and large language models in plain words, including prompts, tokens, and hallucination.

type of session: theory

topics be covered: What AI is versus traditional software; what generative AI is; what an LLM is; next-word prediction; tokens; hallucination; prompts at introduction level; what these tools are good at and weak at.


detailed subtopics to be covered:
Traditional software versus generative software, with a comparison diagram
What artificial intelligence means, including tools that only label
What generative AI means when the output is new text
What a large language model is, without model internals
Next-word prediction and a token as a small piece of text
What a prompt is, and how a clearer request differs from a vague one
Hallucination, and what these tools are good at versus weak at
Student activities: classify tools, continue text, spot a hallucination, and rewrite a prompt
