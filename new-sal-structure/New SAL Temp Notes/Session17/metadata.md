title: Excel — Functions in Excel

objective: Write formulas that start with =, use relative and absolute references, and calculate with SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, MIN, MAX, and a simple IF, then fill the formula down.

type of session: mixture of theory + implementation

topics be covered:
What a formula is and that it starts with =; cell references and ranges; relative versus absolute references ($A$1); SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, MIN, and MAX; a simple IF; filling a formula down on a small marks sheet.

detailed subtopics to be covered:
A formula calculates from cells and begins with the equals sign; the formula bar shows the formula while the cell shows the result
Cell references and inclusive ranges, including why plain numbers are required
Relative references that shift on fill, and absolute references such as $E$1 that stay locked
SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, MIN, and MAX on a four-student marks sheet, including blanks versus zeros
A simple IF that returns Pass or Fail against a locked pass mark
Fill down with the fill handle, Ctrl+D, or Home > Fill > Down, and a short expense activity that locks one rate
